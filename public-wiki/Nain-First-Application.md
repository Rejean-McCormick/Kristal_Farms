# Nain: First Application

The inherited partner package identifies **Nain, Nunatsiavut** as the first target for detailed pre-feasibility.

## Why Nain

The project documentation highlights a combination of:

- northern coastal setting;
- marine logistics;
- community heating demand;
- diesel-displacement potential;
- potential local hydro development;
- a village-edge/port-area compute model;
- relevance as a first Labrador coast demonstration.

## Preliminary hydro concept

The project inventory describes a preliminary Fraser River concept in the approximate **15–20 MW** range. This is a screening basis, not a construction-ready resource estimate.

It still requires confirmation of:

- hydrology and firm winter power;
- fish and environmental impacts;
- rights and community process;
- civil and electrical engineering;
- cost;
- local feeder route;
- fibre availability and redundancy;
- pad-yard location;
- heat users;
- logistics;
- tenant demand.

## Why the first site matters

Nain is not meant to prove that every northern community should host compute. It is intended to test whether the **full Kristal Farms stack** can work in one place and then become a repeatable standard.
