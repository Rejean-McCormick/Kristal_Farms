# Glossary

**Black-box tenancy** — A hosting model in which the infrastructure operator provides physical services without accessing tenant data, models, software or application content.

**Compute pad** — A prepared modular location with standardized power, cooling, heat and fibre interfaces for a containerized compute unit.

**Digital export** — Exporting value through compute services/results over fibre rather than exporting the electricity itself.

**Dry cooler** — An air-cooled heat-rejection unit used as backup or supplemental cooling without evaporative water consumption.

**FPIC** — Free, Prior and Informed Consent.

**Heat-first** — The operational principle that useful heat reuse takes priority over heat rejection.

**HUF** — Heat Utilization Factor: the share of available server heat that is used productively.

**Kristal** — A reusable, versioned, validated knowledge capsule in the proposed knowledge commons.

**MV** — Medium voltage.

**NOC** — Network Operations Centre.

**PUE** — Power Usage Effectiveness: total facility energy divided by IT energy.

**Reuse → Store → Reject** — The thermal dispatch hierarchy used by the project.

**Subsea fibre** — Fibre-optic cable installed in a marine environment.

**WUE** — Water Usage Effectiveness: water consumption per unit of IT energy.
