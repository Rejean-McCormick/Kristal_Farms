# Harmonious Deployment

Kristal Farms is designed to grow through **learning stages**, not through one irreversible megaproject decision.

The deployment principle in the source plan is:

> **Start small, learn fast, train local teams, document results, and scale only where technical, economic, environmental and community conditions support it.**

## Possible sequence

A site may progress through:

1. community process and local capability building;
2. early monitoring and enabling infrastructure;
3. a first renewable asset or construction-energy layer;
4. site preparation, fibre and telemetry;
5. small hydro or other renewable integration;
6. first modular compute pads;
7. heat recovery and local infrastructure;
8. post-mortem review;
9. expansion, adjustment, relocation or stop.

The supplied deployment concept suggests coastal wind as a possible early bootstrap and training layer at some sites. It is **not mandatory for every Kristal Farms site**.

## Multiple stopping points

After each phase, the project should be able to decide:

- repeat;
- adjust;
- scale;
- relocate;
- reduce;
- stop.

That reversibility is a core part of the project's risk-control philosophy.
