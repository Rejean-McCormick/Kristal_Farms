# Metrics and Public Dashboard

Kristal Farms is intended to be measured, not just described.

The internal reference proposes a small stable set of metrics covering efficiency, heat, reliability, environmental performance and community value.

## Core technical metrics

- **PUE — Power Usage Effectiveness**
- **WUE — Water Usage Effectiveness**
- **HUF — Heat Utilization Factor**
- useful heat delivered (MWhth)
- diesel/fuel avoided
- pad occupancy
- power and pad uptime
- fibre availability
- latency and network performance
- environmental temperature-limit compliance

## Community and capability metrics

- local jobs;
- training hours and certifications;
- local procurement;
- buildings connected to heat;
- greenhouse output where relevant;
- grievances and resolution time;
- public reporting cadence.

## Public reporting

The design proposes near-real-time or monthly dashboard updates, quarterly governance review and annual public reporting. Tenant data and personal information remain outside the public dashboard.
