# Human Infrastructure

The long-term project vision extends beyond machines and utilities.

If compute and energy infrastructure create durable economic value, Kristal Farms proposes studying better northern living infrastructure, including:

- housing;
- health services;
- indoor civic space;
- education and training;
- food production and greenhouses;
- cultural and recreational facilities;
- workshops;
- mobility and sheltered connections where justified.

## Designing for the climate

The project documentation rejects the idea of simply moving a southern urban design north. Indoor public environments can be designed as a core part of life in harsh periods, while schedules for work, school and community activities can remain flexible enough to take advantage of good weather and daylight.

## Avoiding the company-town trap

Housing, employment, education, immigration support, health information and grievance decisions should not all be controlled by one project authority.

The long-term principle is:

> **Build infrastructure that increases people's options rather than infrastructure that captures them.**
