# Public Repository and Confidence Levels

Kristal Farms is intended to be developed transparently.

A public repository can include unfinished research without pretending it is final.

## Suggested confidence labels

### Design principle
A deliberate part of the architecture or governance model.

### Working assumption
A number or condition used to test economics or engineering.

### Screening evidence
Information useful for deciding what deserves deeper study.

### Strategic hypothesis
A direction worth investigating but not yet proven.

### Validated site fact
A fact supported by current site-specific engineering, legal, environmental, commercial or community evidence.

### Operational result
Measured performance from a functioning project.

The goal is not to remove uncertainty from the repository. It is to **label uncertainty correctly**.
