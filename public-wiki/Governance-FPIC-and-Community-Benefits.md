# Governance, FPIC and Community Benefits

Kristal Farms treats governance as part of the infrastructure.

## Community process

The baseline partner model requires a community-first process and uses **Free, Prior and Informed Consent (FPIC)** as a governing principle where applicable. The first-phase documentation treats community consent and a Community Benefits / Impact Benefit Agreement as gates before operation.

## Proposed governance bodies

The detailed model includes:

- **Project Council** — overall strategy, budgets and major issues;
- **Heat Committee** — seasonal heat priorities and heat-use performance;
- **Environment Committee** — discharge limits, water/environmental monitoring and incidents;
- **Kristals Council** — public-interest knowledge topics and validation.

## Expansion is not automatic

Additional compute pads should be reviewed against:

- renewable capacity;
- heat offtake;
- summer rejection capacity;
- fibre capacity and redundancy;
- environmental results;
- jobs and training commitments;
- unresolved grievances;
- tenant compliance;
- emergency readiness.

A community/partner process can approve, condition, delay, limit or reject expansion.
