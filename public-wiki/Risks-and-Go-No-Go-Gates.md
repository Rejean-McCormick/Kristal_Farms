# Risks and Go/No-Go Gates

Kristal Farms is designed around formal gates. A site or expansion phase should stop if critical conditions are not met.

## Core gates

### Community and rights

- consent/process completed where required;
- benefit agreements in place;
- governance bodies seated;
- unresolved rights issues addressed.

### Energy and electrical

- resource validated;
- seasonal/firm-power profile understood;
- local load protected;
- substation and protection systems commissioned.

### Cooling and heat

- non-contact loops pressure-tested;
- environmental temperature limits demonstrated;
- useful heat sinks confirmed;
- backup rejection available;
- heat-first controls operational.

### Fibre

- latency and availability tested;
- dual-link failover proven;
- tenant/community network separation demonstrated.

### Safety and security

- electrical, mechanical and fire requirements passed;
- black-box separation audited;
- emergency procedures tested.

### Economics

- cost advantage remains credible after site-specific engineering;
- tenant and heat demand match phased capacity;
- lifecycle obligations and restoration are funded.

The point of a gate is not to make every site pass. It is to make failure visible early enough to stop or redesign.
