# Frequently Asked Questions

## Is Kristal Farms just a data centre?

No. The model integrates renewable energy, compute, fibre, cooling, useful heat, community infrastructure, governance and staged deployment.

## Why not simply transmit the electricity south?

For remote sites, long transmission can become an enormous infrastructure project. Kristal Farms tests whether consuming the power locally and exporting digital value can produce a much lower total infrastructure burden.

## Are you claiming this is cheaper everywhere?

No. Kristal Farms is designed to **select sites where it is significantly better**. A site that cannot demonstrate a strong stacked advantage should not advance.

## Does the project build on land with "low biodiversity"?

That is not the criterion. The criterion is low ecological sensitivity, low habitat conflict and preferably previously disturbed land. Some northern ecosystems are extremely sensitive and should be excluded.

## Does the host see tenant data?

Under the black-box tenancy model, no. The host operates physical infrastructure and monitors physical/network-health metrics, while the tenant retains control of data, models, logs, software and packet content.

## Is server heat really useful?

Yes, if appropriate heat users are close enough and temperatures match. The design prioritizes buildings, hot-water preheat, greenhouses and storage before rejecting heat.

## Why put compute near a village instead of at the river?

Because heat loses value over distance. The preferred architecture keeps compute close to heat users while keeping the electrical connection short enough to avoid a long HV export system.

## Does Kristal Farms require new large dams?

No. The partner model is explicitly not a mega-dam strategy. The deployment plan can consider existing hydro, micro/small hydro, run-of-river systems, wind-hydro hybrids or other renewable configurations if they pass the required gates.

## Is subsea fibre already planned?

It is a strategic opportunity, not a validated route. Any marine cable requires carrier, seabed, landing, regulatory, environmental and rights-holder work.

## Are decommissioned mines part of the main architecture?

No. They are a separate exploratory high-security site archetype.

## Is Nain approved for construction?

No. Nain is the first target for validation. The preliminary hydro concept, fibre, heat users, permits, community process, economics and engineering still require confirmation.

## Is Kristal Farms already an international university?

No. The long-term vision includes training, research partnerships and possibly future accredited university institutions only after formal academic and legal requirements are met.

## Is the project political or separatist?

No. The repository explicitly excludes separatism, border revision, sovereignty disputes and territorial-status strategies from the Kristal Farms project scope.
