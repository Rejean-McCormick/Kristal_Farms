## Important source rule

Some research extracts contain preliminary or imperfect citations. They can inform research but should not automatically be treated as independently verified public evidence. The wiki therefore emphasizes the **project model and its documented reasoning**, while keeping site-specific claims and numeric estimates visibly provisional.
