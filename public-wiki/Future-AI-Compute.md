# Future AI Compute

AI and scientific computing are becoming increasingly power-intensive. Kristal Farms is designed to investigate whether northern renewable-energy sites can host part of that future demand.

## Why the architecture may fit AI

- large electrical load can be placed close to energy;
- liquid cooling is compatible with high-density accelerators;
- modular pads allow phased capacity additions;
- black-box tenancy supports external tenants;
- higher-assurance security options can be added for sensitive workloads;
- fibre exports the work product;
- heat can be reused locally.

## What is not yet proven

The repository does not yet establish:

- future regional AI market share;
- committed tenants;
- exact rack densities;
- required total MW/GW capacity;
- future accelerator economics;
- long-term fibre demand;
- the competitiveness of every site against other global compute regions.

Future AI demand is therefore a **strategic market driver to validate**, not a guaranteed revenue forecast.
