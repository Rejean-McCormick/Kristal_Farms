# Site Archetypes

The detailed internal reference focuses on a community-integrated hydro site, but the broader platform vision identifies several possible site classes.

## 1. Community-integrated node

Renewable energy, modular compute, heat recovery, local fibre and community infrastructure are tightly connected. This is the baseline and most developed archetype.

## 2. Coastal connectivity node

A site where marine logistics and possible terrestrial/subsea fibre access create additional value.

## 3. Secure brownfield or mine node

A separate high-security archetype using hardened or decommissioned industrial infrastructure. Heat reuse may be less community-centred, and dedicated engineering is required.

## 4. Regional node

A larger compute site considered only where energy, fibre, environmental conditions, rights-holder/community process and economics support it.

## Common rule

Different archetypes may share standardized power, cooling, fibre, telemetry, tenancy and governance interfaces without forcing every site into the same physical layout.
