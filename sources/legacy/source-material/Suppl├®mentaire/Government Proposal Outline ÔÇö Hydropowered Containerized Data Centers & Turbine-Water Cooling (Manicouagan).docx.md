### **1\. Executive Summary**

* **Project Name:** Hydropowered Containerized Data Centers  
* **Location:** Near Hydroelectric Dams on the Manicouagan River, Quebec  
* **Objective:** Establish containerized data centers leveraging the cold discharge water from hydroelectric turbines for energy-efficient cooling, supporting sustainable digital infrastructure.  
* **Key Benefits:** Reduced energy consumption, low environmental impact, support for digital economy growth, and alignment with Canada's sustainability goals.

---

### **2\. Problem Statement**

* **Global Data Demand:** Rising demand for data processing and storage due to cloud computing, AI, and IoT.  
* **Energy Consumption:** Data centers are among the largest consumers of electricity globally, contributing significantly to carbon emissions.  
* **Cooling Challenges:** Traditional data center cooling systems are energy-intensive and environmentally unsustainable.  
* **Opportunity in Quebec:** The Manicouagan River hydroelectric dams provide abundant renewable energy and cold water, ideal for sustainable data center cooling.

---

### **3\. Project Goals and Objectives**

* **Primary Goals:**  
  * Build containerized data centers powered by hydroelectric energy.  
  * Utilize cold discharge water for cost-effective and eco-friendly cooling.  
* **Objectives:**  
  * Create a modular, scalable data center infrastructure.  
  * Minimize carbon footprint and operational costs.  
  * Boost local economic development by creating high-tech jobs.

---

### **4\. Project Description**

#### **A. Infrastructure**

* **Containerized Data Centers:**  
  * Modular, pre-built server farms housed in shipping containers.  
  * Rapid deployment and scalability.  
* **Hydroelectric Integration:**  
  * Energy supplied directly from the hydroelectric dam.  
  * Cooling system using discharge water from the turbines.

#### **B. Cooling Technology**

* **Heat Exchangers:** Closed-loop system transferring heat from servers to turbine water without contamination.  
* **Direct Liquid Cooling:** Coolant circulates directly through server racks, with heat dissipated via turbine water.  
* **Environmental Safeguards:** Return water temperature controlled to avoid disrupting aquatic ecosystems.

#### **C. Site Selection**

* **Proximity to Dams:** Location near the Manicouagan River hydroelectric facilities for minimal energy loss and reduced water transport costs.  
* **Accessibility:** Road and utility access for easy deployment and maintenance.  
* **Environmental Impact Study:** Ensure minimal ecological disturbance.

#### **D. Sustainability Features**

* 100% renewable hydroelectric energy.  
* Zero reliance on fossil fuels for cooling.  
* Low water and energy consumption per unit of compute power.
