# Owner direction — Pass 14 economic proof

Pass 14 is the final broad research pass. Its purpose is to test the structural economics of moving digital value rather than defaulting to long roads and long high-voltage export from remote northern generation.

The comparison must not assume Kristal always wins. It must preserve site-specific unknowns, community priority, fibre requirements, port/local access, local electrical works, serviced-pad costs, regulation and financing.

After Pass 14, work should shift from horizontal research passes to corridor/site project dossiers.
