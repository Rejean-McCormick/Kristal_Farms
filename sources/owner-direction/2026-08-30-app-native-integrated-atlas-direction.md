# Owner Direction — App-native integrated atlas

**Captured:** 2026-08-30  
**Authority:** project/application architecture direction, not external technical evidence.

The Kristal Geospatial Platform is data-driven, uses PostGIS as operational source of truth, separates geography from evidence/observations/scenarios, and keeps screening unranked. Pass 13 should connect hydrology, community, logistics, telecom, energy, environment and governance context through canonical IDs and provenance rather than forcing all knowledge into map geometry.

Public Showcase data must be a versioned release; live research may evolve independently. Community priority, planning-margin != compute-capacity, external-reference != Kristal-candidate and no-ranking rules remain mandatory.
