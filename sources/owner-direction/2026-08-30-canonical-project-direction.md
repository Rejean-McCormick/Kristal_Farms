# Project Direction — Canonical Kristal Farms Architecture

**Captured:** 2026-08-30
**Authority:** project direction; authoritative for intended architecture, not independent technical/economic/environmental evidence.

Kristal Farms is focused on northern Québec and Labrador and is primarily a **hydro-resource access architecture**.

Core direction:

- bring flexible compute to remote renewable energy;
- consume electricity locally;
- export digital value by fibre;
- treat avoided/reduced long roads and high-voltage corridors as a primary structural value proposition;
- use Côte-Nord as a pilot/learning geography while keeping farther-north Québec and Labrador as the long-term resource geography;
- keep generation-side, community/port-side and split compute layouts available;
- use serviced compute sites/pads as the core commercial hosting model;
- protect community and critical loads ahead of flexible compute;
- treat heat as an optional co-product rather than a universal siting rule;
- use marine logistics where it can replace part of the road burden;
- keep screening unranked until transparent methodology and governance authorize ranking;
- preserve anti-capture and separated-authority principles as successful nodes create land/infrastructure value;
- develop project-specific corridor/site dossiers after broad regional research.
