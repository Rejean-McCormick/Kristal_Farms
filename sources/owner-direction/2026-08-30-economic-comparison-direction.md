# Project Direction — Economic Architecture Comparison

**Captured:** 2026-08-30
**Authority:** project research direction, not independent cost evidence.

Economic work should test the structural consequences of moving digital value rather than defaulting to long roads and long high-voltage electrical export from remote generation.

The comparison must not assume Kristal Farms always wins. Site-specific unknowns must remain explicit, including community priority, fibre, port/local access, local electrical works, serviced-pad costs, regulation, O&M and financing.

Completed-project ratios and public funding intensities are research benchmarks only. Project economics begin only when both alternatives are priced on equivalent scope.
