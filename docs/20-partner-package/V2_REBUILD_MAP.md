# Partner Package v2 — Rebuild Map

**Status:** Controlled migration plan. Existing PDFs/DOCX remain v1 until regenerated and approved.

## Objective

Rebuild the partner package so the external story reflects the v2 platform thesis **without overstating hypotheses** and without losing the concrete Nain-first application.

## Narrative hierarchy for v2

1. **Platform thesis:** bring compute to difficult-to-export renewable energy; export value by fibre.
2. **Differentiators:** cold climate, heat reuse, black-box security, modularity/reversibility, low-impact siting discipline.
3. **Strategic extensions:** subsea/coastal fibre, secure brownfield/mine archetype, northern fibre network, accumulated northern expertise.
4. **First application:** Nain/Labrador.
5. **Evidence gates:** hydrology, environment, community/rights, fibre, security, economics and tenant demand.

## Claims that must remain qualified in v2

Until validated, use formulations such as:

- “may reduce / has the potential to reduce long-distance transmission requirements”;
- “evaluate lower lifecycle footprint against a defined transmission alternative”;
- “coastal sites may offer subsea-fibre landing opportunities”;
- “decommissioned mines are being screened as a potential higher-security archetype”;
- “Québec and Labrador may be well positioned to serve future AI compute demand.”

Do **not** state these as settled outcomes.
