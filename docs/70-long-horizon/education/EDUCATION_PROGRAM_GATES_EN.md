# Kristal Farms — Education & University Program Gates

**Status:** Working implementation framework  
**Purpose:** Prevent aspirational education language from outrunning legal, academic, community and operational readiness.

## Gate 0 — Concept / no public institution claim

Allowed:

- research agenda;
- skills inventory;
- discussions with academic/technical partners;
- curriculum concepts;
- infrastructure planning.

Not allowed:

- claiming university status;
- promising recognized degrees;
- advertising unapproved immigration/study pathways;
- setting international-student quotas without capacity analysis.

## Gate 1 — Technical training pilot

Minimum evidence:

- defined competencies and instructors;
- safety and safeguarding procedures;
- insurance/liability review;
- learning records and completion criteria;
- host-community access and participation plan;
- Human Dignity Framework review;
- language-access plan;
- funding and operating owner.

## Gate 2 — Academic / research partnership

Minimum evidence:

- written MOU or agreement with qualified institution(s);
- research-governance and ethics process;
- IP/publication/confidentiality rules;
- student/researcher supervision;
- data/privacy architecture;
- academic and community conflict-of-interest controls.

## Gate 3 — Visiting international learner program

Minimum evidence:

- lawful study/work/visitor pathways as applicable;
- transparent admission and fees/funding;
- housing, health, transport and emergency capacity;
- orientation to host community, language and rights;
- portable learning records;
- return/transfer support;
- independent complaint and safeguarding channels;
- no dependency on one institution for all housing/employment/status/remedy functions.

## Gate 4 — Recognized joint credentials

Minimum evidence:

- credentialing authority identified;
- program and assessment standards approved;
- recognition/transferability mapping;
- qualified faculty/instructors;
- academic appeal and integrity rules;
- financial sustainability;
- external quality assurance.

## Gate 5 — University institution

The project may describe an institution as a university only when the relevant jurisdictional requirements and authorizations are satisfied.

Minimum program controls should include:

- legal/degree-granting/accreditation authority;
- independent academic governance;
- sustainable faculty and research capacity;
- admissions, assessment, appeals and academic-integrity systems;
- stable operating finance and student-support services;
- library/data/research infrastructure;
- research ethics and publication governance;
- host-community representation;
- language charter in force;
- Human Dignity Framework operationalized;
- external review and public accountability appropriate to the institution.

## Cross-gate rule

A later gate never cancels an earlier safeguard. Growth must increase academic capability **without reducing community consent, cultural autonomy, individual rights, student portability or the real ability to leave**.
