# Northern Mine Reuse — Research Inventory

**As of:** 2026-08-31  
**Status:** C4 exploratory inventory. **Unranked. No site below is selected or represented as available to Kristal Farms.**

## Purpose

This inventory records mine types and reference cases relevant to two questions:

1. can existing underground/industrial mine infrastructure reduce new-build scope for compute or supporting infrastructure?;
2. can an open pit — including an old/historical pit — be reused as part of a pumped-storage system?

The inventory is intentionally conservative. A mine name is a research lead, not a project opportunity.

## Northern Québec / Labrador research objects

| Research object | Geography | Lifecycle evidence | Reuse relevance | Current constraint / interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Renard mine** | Eeyou Istchee James Bay, Québec | Operations ended Jan. 2025; care & maintenance in 2025. Court-approved Li-FT option announced July 2026. | Strong modern **underground/industrial infrastructure analogue**. Historical ramp, electrical, pumping, hoisting, camp and powerhouse evidence exists. | **Current-use/acquisition conflict.** Treat as reference/comparable unless ownership situation changes; not an available Kristal site claim. |
| **Schefferville abandoned mine cluster** (Burnt Creek, Ferriman, Fleming, French, Gagnon, Knox, Retty, Rowe, Star Creek and others) | Schefferville / Labrador Trough, Québec side | Historical iron operations mainly 1953–1982; multiple sites remain on Québec abandoned-mine lists. | **Old open-pit reservoir/brownfield research**. | **Lac Jeannine** | Côte-Nord, Québec | Historical iron mine, 1961–1976; on abandoned-mine list. | Large historical open-pit reference for **reservoir geometry / brownfield reuse**. | No claim yet on usable volume, head, grid value, sealing or environmental acceptability. |
| **Asbestos Hill / Purtuniq** | Nunavik, Québec | Historical asbestos mine; State restoration responsibility/reference. | Geometry/remote-mine infrastructure **reference only**; in principle old pits remain eligible for reservoir research. | **Major asbestos/environmental constraint.** Do not promote as storage/compute opportunity without exceptional environmental evidence and governance review. |

## External technical reference cases

| Reference | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Kidston, Queensland, Australia** | Demonstrates that an **old abandoned mine** can supply the upper and lower reservoirs of a 250 MW / 2,000 MWh pumped-storage project. This is the principal precedent supporting inclusion of old pits in Kristal Farms reservoir research. |
| **Okinawa Yanbaru, Japan** | Demonstrated seawater pumped storage with the ocean as lower reservoir (30 MW, ~136 m effective head). Useful for corrosion, lining and marine-system questions; not a currently operating template. |

## Research tasks

For each northern mine research object, collect source-backed values before any project-level comparison:

- exact mine/pit polygons and DEM-derived elevations;
- pit water level and bathymetry/volume where available;
- underground access/condition and dewatering state where relevant;
- current owner, mining titles and restoration responsibility;
- contamination/ARD and water-quality evidence;
- current power infrastructure and **available** capacity (not historical demand);
- fibre/telecom evidence;
- road/rail/air/port access;
- community/Indigenous rights and governance context;
- current competing redevelopment plans.

Do not rank until project governance explicitly changes `ranking_allowed`.
