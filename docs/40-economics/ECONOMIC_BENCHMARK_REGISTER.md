# Economic Benchmark Register

Benchmarks are stored in `research.economic_benchmark`. They are research references and carry `usable_as_site_estimate=false` unless a later project-specific process explicitly validates their applicability.

## Transmission

- Hydro-Québec Micoua–Saguenay — 262 km; published total project cost C$1.271B.
- Hydro-Québec Chamouchouane–Bout-de-l’Île — 420 km main line + 19 km segment plus Judith-Jasmin substation; published total project cost C$1.3B.

These are project-total ratios with different scopes, not universal line-cost rates.

## Road

- Allnorth North Labrador pre-feasibility — approximately 809 km of new road; ROM C$2.1B; paving identified as an additional C$600M; annual maintenance approximately C$13M in the study.

This is a planning benchmark, not a route estimate for Kristal Farms.

## Fibre

- CRTC/KRG Nunavik — up to C$79.419M for 933 km of transport fibre.
- CRTC/Government of Nunavut — up to C$271.937M for 1,300 km of transport fibre.

These figures are public funding contributions and are stored as **funding-intensity proxies**, not fibre construction unit costs.

## Data-centre context

- Uptime 2025 provides contemporary PUE context; Kristal Farms does not assign itself a project PUE without design evidence.
- Québec data-centre tariff treatment remains a regulatory input to resolve for any applicable project. No exemption for dedicated or local generation is assumed.
