# Economic Architecture Frontier

## Purpose

The economic comparison tests Kristal Farms' structural thesis without turning generic benchmarks into site estimates. It compares the enabling infrastructure associated with two monetization paths for remote generation:

1. **electrical export:** remote generation → access infrastructure → transmission/substations → external load;
2. **digital export:** remote generation → protected local interface → flexible compute → fibre export.

The available evidence supports a **research thesis**, not a bankable savings claim. Completed Québec transmission projects and northern road studies show that long terrestrial infrastructure can be capital intensive. Northern fibre funding decisions provide useful context but are funding proxies rather than complete construction-cost curves.

This difference is sufficient to justify project-level testing of the core inversion. It does **not** establish that Kristal Farms is cheaper at every location.

## Generic distance frontier

The current non-site stress grid spans:

- HV export: 25 / 50 / 100 / 200 km;
- export road: 10 / 25 / 50 / 100 km;
- fibre: 100 / 250 / 500 / 933 km.

Using the lower transmission/road reference against the higher fibre proxy, **60 of 64** generic combinations leave positive budget headroom before all unpriced Kristal Farms-specific infrastructure. Four do not. Under the opposite reference combination, all 64 are positive.

These combinations are sensitivity cases, **not a statistical sample of sites and not a probability of success**.

## What remains unpriced

The generic frontier deliberately leaves project-specific items unpriced until a corridor or site dossier provides evidence, including:

- serviced compute pads;
- local MV/substation/switchgear;
- port upgrades;
- fibre shore-end, landing and terminal works;
- local access;
- storage or backup where required;
- heat recovery where justified;
- full operations and maintenance;
- taxes, financing and contingency;
- losses and grid reinforcement;
- project-specific environmental, rights and permitting costs.

A positive difference is therefore called **remaining unpriced Kristal Farms budget**, not savings.
