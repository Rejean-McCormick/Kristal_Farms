# Economic Scenario Policy

1. A benchmark is not a site cost.
2. Unknown cost is `UNPRICED`, never zero.
3. A positive reference frontier is not called savings.
4. Generation CAPEX is held common in the architecture-differential test unless project engineering shows that the generation concept itself changes.
5. Tenant compute hardware is not counted as avoided infrastructure.
6. Project NPV/IRR is blocked until a site dossier has sufficiently complete engineering and commercial inputs.
7. No site ranking is enabled.
8. Regulatory treatment of dedicated or local generation remains an explicit project-level question.
9. Every scenario result must retain dataset, model and assumption lineage.
10. Sensitivity cases are not probabilities and must not be presented as a success rate.
