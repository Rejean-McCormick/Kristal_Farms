# Manual Review Gate for River Reaches

A hydrography candidate may be accepted as a connected research reach only after review confirms:

1. candidate geometry comes from the authoritative source for the jurisdiction;
2. it intersects or is hydrologically consistent with the WSC station location (allowing known cartographic offsets);
3. the candidate belongs to the intended watercourse/system, not a nearby tributary or lake centerline;
4. connectivity across segment boundaries is demonstrated;
5. upstream/downstream orientation is established;
6. source fields and OBJECTIDs/graph identifiers are preserved;
7. ambiguous alternatives are not silently discarded;
8. the accepted line is labelled a **research reach**, not a dam alignment.

Only an accepted reach may be passed to the HRDEM terrain script.
