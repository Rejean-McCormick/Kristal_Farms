Promotion means deeper study, not ranking or selection. |

## Decision record format

When a decision is made, record:

- decision ID and date;
- decision and rationale;
- approver/authority;
- evidence reviewed;
- affected code/data/docs;
- follow-up actions;
- superseded decision, if any.
