# Strategic Principles — Current

The current principles are taken from the 2026-08-18 English public wiki and the technical source set it summarizes.

## Core inversion

> **Bring compute to renewable energy, consume the electricity locally, export digital value by fibre, and reuse the heat.**

Conventional direction:

**Remote generation → long electrical corridor → distant load**

Kristal Farms direction:

**Renewable resource → short local electrical connection → modular compute → fibre export**

## Structural advantage is a qualifying requirement

A site should advance only if the full system demonstrates a compelling advantage after accounting for:

- generation;
- local power connection;
- compute infrastructure;
- cooling and heat recovery;
- fibre;
- logistics;
- environmental effects;
- community process;
- reliability;
- lifetime economics.

## Heat-first

> **Reuse → Store → Reject**

Useful heat is prioritized before storage or rejection, with compute expansion matched to validated heat sinks and safe rejection capacity.

## Fibre is the export corridor

The project moves digital value over fibre rather than assuming that bulk electricity must be moved over very long distances.

## Black-box tenancy

The host manages physical infrastructure interfaces. The tenant controls its servers, software, models, datasets, application logs, encryption, workload scheduling and internal cybersecurity.

## Environmental selection

Use **low ecological sensitivity and low habitat conflict**, not “low biodiversity,” as the site-selection language. Prefer existing or previously disturbed industrial/port land where practical. Site exclusion is part of the model.

## Harmonious deployment

> **Start small, learn fast, train local teams, document results, and scale only where technical, economic, environmental and community conditions support it.**

Expansion can repeat, adjust, scale, relocate, reduce or stop.
