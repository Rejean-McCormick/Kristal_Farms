## Rules

- Searchable extraction does not replace the original source.
- Raw datasets are never silently overwritten by cleaned/derived data.
- Formatted partner files are not duplicated into a separate source folder because their partner-package location is their preserved operational home.
- Legacy material remains under `sources/legacy/`.

## Pass 9 hydro-atlas sources

Controlled registry: `data/processed/pass9/sources_pass9.json`.

Priority source stack:

1. WSC station metadata + WSC hydrometric basin polygons;
2. GRHQ for Québec connected hydrography;
3. Canada1Water/NHN for national/cross-border stream topology;
4. HRDEM Mosaic for terrain after connected flowline ingestion;
5. supplied legacy village/Labrador hydro files only for contextual joins.

WSC explicitly labels the drainage-basin polygon layer prerelease. Pass 9 therefore keeps station drainage-area metadata separate and does not recompute or silently replace it from unavailable polygons.

## Pass 14 economic benchmark source rules

- Hydro-Québec completed-project totals are divided by published line length only as **project-total/line-km ratios**, never line-only costs.
- Allnorth North Labrador road figures are ROM pre-feasibility estimates and remain scope-specific.
- CRTC northern fibre amounts are approved contributions, not proven total project costs.
- Uptime PUE values are industry context, not Kristal design targets.
- R-4333-2026 is treated as an active regulatory docket; no final tariff or exemption is assumed.

All app-native economic benchmarks carry `usable_as_site_estimate=false`.
