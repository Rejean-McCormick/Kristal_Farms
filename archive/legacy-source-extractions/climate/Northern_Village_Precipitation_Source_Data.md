---

\## 1. Use and limitations

\- This is a \*\*screening document only\*\*. It records the precipitation data currently used in the village inventory and separates local station values from regional proxy assignments.

\- \*\*Rainfall\*\* means liquid precipitation only, in millimetres per year.

\- \*\*Total precipitation\*\* means rainfall plus snow water-equivalent, as reported by climate normals or by the cited source.

\- Hydropower screening should \*\*not\*\* rank sites by rainfall alone. The next screen must include watershed area, usable head/drop, winter low-flow, snowpack timing, permafrost, lake storage, port/fibre access, and FPIC/community readiness.

\- \*\*Hopedale is flagged as a working value\*\*. Verify it directly in ECCC bulk CSV before design or investment use.
