**Status:** Control map for rebuilding external partner materials around the full platform thesis.

The numbered `00–14` package remains the inherited v1 Nain/Labrador set. `v2-working/` captured the first platform reframing. `v3-working/` adds the human-infrastructure, international-learning and dignity-governance layers without treating them as already operational programs.

## v3 rule

Partner-facing material must distinguish clearly between:

1. **operating architecture already developed in detail**;
2. **site hypotheses requiring engineering and permitting**;
3. **platform strategy requiring market/partner validation**;
4. **long-term human/education vision requiring separate governance, legal, academic and community approvals**.

No polished deliverable should compress those four maturity levels into one undifferentiated promise.
