# Research History — Passes 1–14

This is a concise handoff history, not a substitute for the underlying evidence.

## Passes 1–5 — foundation and evidence discipline

Built the initial structured map/research approach: communities, rivers, ports, infrastructure, hydrometric references, recovered 2026 legacy map layers, port-capability research, topography/head safeguards and historical Labrador hydro-economic evidence. The key discipline established here was **do not turn a point into a site, a port into heavy-lift capability, terrain drop into project head, or historical cost into current economics**.

## Pass 6 — corridor organization

Added conceptual corridor groupings, straight-line research relationships, current infrastructure-status corrections and evidence-coverage metadata. Corridors were deliberately represented as research groupings, not routes.

## Pass 7 — deeper marine / sealift / telecom evidence

Deepened Nunavik marine evidence, sealift booking/schedule evidence, North Labrador telecom status conflicts, and real-project logistics precedents. Managed marine infrastructure was separated from heavy-project-cargo capability.

## Pass 8 — energy systems / hosting architecture

Reset the electrical framing: existing autonomous-grid planning margins are not multi-MW compute capacity. The active model became **new remote generation + protected community interface + flexible compute + fibre export**. Innavik, Lac-Robertson, Quaqtaq/Puvirnituq, La Romaine and Nain Wind were used as external reference cases, not Kristal site selections.

## Pass 9 — Hydro Resource Atlas

Created a 24-river/station research atlas and source hierarchy for WSC basins, GRHQ/Canada1Water hydrography and HRDEM terrain. No synthetic basins, river reaches, head, design flow or MW were created.

## Pass 10 — official geometry / terrain pipeline

Operationalized retrieval/review scripts and mandatory gates for official basin geometry, connected hydrography and HRDEM terrain profiles. Query windows became system/research objects rather than pseudo-geographic facts.

## Pass 11 — app-native hydrology foundation

Migrated the research model toward canonical PostGIS entities, relations, evidence, observations and ingestion jobs. GeoJSON became a publish/research artifact rather than the intended operational source of truth.

## Pass 12 — hydrology observations

Added HYDAT series/versioning and derivation contracts, algorithm lineage and quality gates. No real HYDAT time series were fabricated when network access prevented materialization.

## Pass 13 — integrated atlas

Joined hydrology, communities, external energy references, marine/telecom context, corridors, evidence and screening state into an app-native atlas. Added a data-driven layer catalog, public publish outputs and Showcase story configuration.

## Pass 14 — economic architecture frontier

Built a reproducible structural break-even engine comparing conventional remote electrical/road infrastructure with local compute + digital export. Unknown Kristal-specific costs remain `UNPRICED`; outputs are frontier/sensitivity results, not bankable project savings or site rankings.

## End state

Pass 14 closes the general horizontal discovery phase. Future work should be corridor/site dossiers with real geometry, hydrology, engineering, environmental/governance work and vendor/utility quotes.
