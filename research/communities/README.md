# Community-context research

Exploratory workspace for community infrastructure/context research before it is normalized, reviewed and promoted into governed data.

Community, Indigenous-rights, privacy and governance constraints still apply here. Research notes do not become product claims without explicit evidence review and publication.

Active KF target-village research lives in `targets/`. It is promoted through `pipelines/publish/build_target_villages_public.py`; product runtime code must not read these YAML files directly.
